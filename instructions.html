General Instructions

  • Total duration of the examination is 180 minutes.
  • A countdown timer in the header shows the remaining time. The test auto-submits when time expires.
  • Click Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next question.
  • Click Mark for Review & Next to flag a question for later review.
  • Click Clear Response to erase your answer for the current question.
  • Click any question number in the right-side palette to jump directly to that question.

Marking Scheme

MCQ
Full marks for correct option. Negative marking: −⅓ marks for 1-mark Qs, −⅔ for 2-mark Qs. No marks for unattempted.
MSQ
Full marks only if all correct options are selected and no incorrect ones. No negative marking.
NAT
Full marks if your value is within the accepted tolerance. No negative marking.
